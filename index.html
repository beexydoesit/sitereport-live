      Questions people ask

      How do I know if a website is down for everyone or just me?

      Type the address above. We check it from our server, not your connection. If we reach it and you can't, the problem is on your end: your network, your DNS, or a browser extension.

      What makes a website look sketchy?

      Brand-new domains, unusual endings like .xyz or .top, prices far below everyone else, no street address or phone number, and payment only by gift card, wire or crypto. Any one of those is a reason to slow down.

      What does the Trust Index mean?

      A 0 to 100 score combining our live check with what's publicly known about who runs the site, how long it has existed, and its record. It's guidance, not a guarantee.

      Is SiteReport.live free?

      Yes. Every check is free and no account is required.
