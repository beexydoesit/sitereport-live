Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026

SiteReport.live ("we," "us") is owned and operated by Brandon Bonds. This policy explains what information we collect when you use sitereport.live, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Website addresses you check. When you search a domain, it is sent to our server so we can test whether the site is online and secure. We do not link searches to your identity.
  • Your email address, only if you subscribe to the SiteReport Outage Alert. We store it through our hosting provider, Netlify.
  • Ratings and comments, including any name you choose to add. Do not include personal details you don't want shown.
  • Messages you send us. The contact form opens your own email app, so we receive whatever you choose to send, like any normal email.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, collected automatically by our hosting provider and advertising partners.

How we use it

We use this information to run site checks, send the monthly Outage Alert, respond to inquiries, prevent spam and abuse, and understand how the site is used so we can improve it. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and advertising

We show ads through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of many third-party vendors at aboutads.info. We also store small amounts of data in your browser (local storage) to remember things like whether you already subscribed.

Affiliate links

Some "Promoted partner" links are affiliate links. If you visit or buy through them, we may earn a commission at no cost to you. Affiliate relationships never change a site's Trust Index.

Accuracy of results

Site reports combine automated checks with public information and are provided for general guidance only. Always use your own judgment before sharing personal or payment information with any website.

Your California privacy rights

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, ask us to delete it, and opt out of its sale. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, email us at the address below.

Unsubscribing and deletion

Every Outage Alert email includes an unsubscribe link. You can also email us anytime to remove your email or comments.

Children

SiteReport.live is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the effective date above.
